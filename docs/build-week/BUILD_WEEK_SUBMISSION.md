## Devpost identity

- **Title:** ProofTwin AI — Evidence-Backed Retail Decision Intelligence
- **Category:** Evidence-backed retail decision intelligence
- **Tagline:** Prove every number. Simulate every decision.
- **One-line pitch:** ProofTwin turns raw store transactions into verified decisions, compares three possible futures, and never spends without human approval.
- **Supporting line:** It does not guess. It proves, simulates, and waits for human approval.

## What judges can verify

ProofTwin AI turns deterministic store transactions into immutable evidence,
uses ProofTwin Copilot only inside bounded read tools, and compares three
server-calculated futures in the ProofTwin Scenario Engine. An owner must
explicitly approve or reject the selected proposal. Approval creates at most
one PurchaseOrder DRAFT; it never orders, pays, receives, changes inventory, or
contacts a supplier.

The public experience remains at `/savdograph`. Internal routes, API endpoints,
permissions, evidence types, prompt versions, calculations, and approval-state
contracts intentionally retain their existing identifiers.
